🫁 Pranayama: Breathing Techniques

Control your breath, control your mind. Calm your nervous system instantly.

Nadi Shodhana

5-10 min

Alternate Nostril Breathing

Benefit: Balances both hemispheres of the brain, calms the nervous system
How to do it:
  1. Sit comfortably with spine straight
  2. Close your right nostril with thumb
  3. Inhale slowly through left nostril
  4. Close left nostril, open right
  5. Exhale through right nostril
  6. Reverse the pattern
  7. Continue for 5-10 minutes

Ujjayi Breathing

5-15 min

Ocean Breath

Benefit: Soothes the mind, reduces anxiety and stress
How to do it:
  1. Sit or lie comfortably
  2. Inhale through your nose
  3. Exhale through mouth with a 'ha' sound
  4. Do this 3-4 times
  5. Gradually close your mouth while exhaling
  6. Continue with nasal breathing
  7. Maintain the ocean-like sound

Sama Vritti

5 min

Equal Breathing

Benefit: Stabilizes the mind, reduces stress and anxiety
How to do it:
  1. Sit comfortably with closed eyes
  2. Count to 4 while inhaling
  3. Hold breath for count of 4
  4. Exhale for count of 4
  5. Keep all counts equal
  6. Continue for 5 minutes

Bhramari

3-5 min

Bee Breath

Benefit: Instantly calms the mind, relieves tension
How to do it:
  1. Sit comfortably with spine straight
  2. Close your eyes
  3. Inhale deeply through both nostrils
  4. Exhale while humming like a bee
  5. Feel the vibration in your head
  6. Repeat 5-10 times

Shitali

5 min

Cooling Breath

Benefit: Reduces body heat, calms agitation
How to do it:
  1. Sit comfortably with spine straight
  2. Curl your tongue (or purse lips if unable)
  3. Inhale deeply through the tongue
  4. Close your mouth
  5. Exhale slowly through your nose
  6. Repeat 10-15 times

4-7-8 Breathing

3-5 min

Deep Relaxation Breath

Benefit: Activates parasympathetic nervous system for deep relaxation
How to do it:
  1. Sit comfortably with closed eyes
  2. Inhale through nose for count of 4
  3. Hold breath for count of 7
  4. Exhale through mouth for count of 8
  5. This is one round
  6. Repeat 4 times

🧘 Meditation Techniques

Find your inner sanctuary. Choose a technique that resonates with you.

🕉️

Mantra Meditation

10-20 minutes

Focuses the mind, reduces stress, brings inner peace

Repeat a sacred mantra like 'Om', 'So-Hum', or 'Aham Brahmasmi' silently. Let it anchor your awareness and guide you to stillness.

💫

Chakra Meditation

15-20 minutes

Balances energy, releases blockages, promotes healing

Focus on each chakra from root to crown, visualizing healing light flowing through your entire being.

🌸

Mindfulness Meditation

10-15 minutes

Increases awareness, reduces stress, improves focus

Observe your thoughts without judgment. Let them pass like clouds drifting across the sky.

❤️

Loving-Kindness Meditation

15 minutes

Cultivates compassion, reduces anxiety and anger

Send wishes of love and peace to yourself, loved ones, and all beings. Feel the warmth in your heart expand.

😴

Yoga Nidra

20-45 minutes

Deep relaxation, healing at all levels

Guided relaxation where you lie still and follow verbal guidance through layers of consciousness. Profoundly restorative.

⚡ Quick Relief Tips

When you need instant calm

🫁

Box Breathing

Inhale-4, Hold-4, Exhale-4, Hold-4. Repeat 5 times. Takes 2 minutes.

🌳

5-4-3-2-1 Grounding

Name 5 things you see, 4 you feel, 3 you hear, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.

💧

Cold Water Face Immersion

Splash cold water on your face. It activates the vagus nerve for instant calm.

🚶

Slow Walk

Take a mindful walk. Feel each step, breathe deeply. 10 minutes transforms mood.

🍃

Progressive Relaxation

Tense and release each muscle group from toes to head. 5 minutes of relief.

🎵

Calming Music

Listen to Vedic chants, nature sounds, or instrumental music. Let vibrations soothe you.

How to Chant Mantras

1

Choose Your Mantra

Select a mantra that resonates with your intention or current need.

2

Find a Sacred Space

Sit in a quiet, clean space facing east or north if possible.

3

Sit Comfortably

Sit with spine straight. You can use a cushion for comfort.

4

Begin Chanting

Chant slowly and clearly. Feel the vibration in your body.

5

108 Repetitions

Chant 108 times (sacred number) or as many times as feels right.

6

Rest in Silence

After chanting, sit in silence for a few minutes. Feel the resonance.
